QUESTIONS

Shop owners ask.

What is Chapa Biz?

Chapa Biz is a business ledger app for small shops in Kenya. It replaces the notebook by recording sales, expenses, stock and customer debts on your phone, in English or Swahili, and it works offline.

How much does Chapa Biz cost?

KSh 50 a day, KSh 300 a week or KSh 1,200 a month. Every plan includes all features. Message us on WhatsApp to be set up and shown how to pay.

Do I need internet?

No. Chapa Biz saves everything on your phone first, so you can keep selling offline. When you're back online it backs up and syncs.

Do I need a smartphone?

Yes, any Android phone or iPhone with a browser. It installs from the web page, so no Play Store is needed.

Can my workers use it?

Yes. Give each worker an invite code and they set their own 4-digit PIN. They only see their shop and never your buying prices or profit.

Is my data safe?

Your records are saved on your phone and backed up to a secure server. Edits keep a full history, so nothing is silently changed.

Can I use it in Swahili?

Yes. The whole app works in English and Swahili, and voice entry understands both, including common Sheng number words.

How do I pay?

Chapa Biz costs KSh 50 a day, KSh 300 a week or KSh 1,200 a month. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll set you up and show you how to pay.

I have more than one shop.

An owner can have several businesses in one account and compare them side by side.
