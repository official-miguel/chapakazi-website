QUESTIONS

Shop owners ask.

What is Chapa Biz?

Chapa Biz is a POS (point of sale) and business ledger app for small shops in Kenya. It replaces the notebook and the POS machine by recording sales, expenses, stock, M-Pesa payments and customer debts on your phone, in English or Swahili, and it works offline.

Is Chapa Biz a POS system?

Yes. Chapa Biz is a mobile POS system: you record every sale on your phone by tapping or speaking, stock drops by itself, and you see sales, profit and debts in one place. It runs on the phone you already have, so you don't need to buy a POS machine.

What is a good POS for a small shop in Kenya?

A small Kenyan shop needs a POS that is cheap, works on a phone, keeps working when the internet goes off, handles M-Pesa and customer credit, and speaks Swahili. Chapa Biz was built for exactly that, from KSh 49 a day with no hardware to buy.

Do I need a POS machine, printer or computer?

No. Chapa Biz runs on any Android phone or iPhone. There is no POS machine, till hardware or computer to buy.

Does Chapa Biz work with M-Pesa?

Yes. You can record M-Pesa sales, and a shop can ask for its Till or Paybill number to be connected so M-Pesa payments arrive in the app by themselves.

How much does Chapa Biz cost?

KSh 49 a day, KSh 299 a week or KSh 1,199 a month for your first shop, plus KSh 29 a day, KSh 179 a week or KSh 699 a month for each extra shop. Every plan includes all features, and new owners get 14 days free.

Do I need internet?

No. Chapa Biz saves everything on your phone first, so you can keep selling offline. When you're back online it backs up and syncs.

What kind of businesses can use it?

Dukas and retail shops, mini marts and small supermarkets, kiosks, mama mbogas, hardware shops, agrovets, cosmetics and boutiques, wines and spirits, salons and barbershops, and similar small businesses in Kenya.

Can my workers use it?

Yes. Give each worker an invite code and they set their own 4-digit PIN. They only see their shop and never your buying prices or profit.

Is my data safe?

Your records are saved on your phone and backed up to a secure server. Edits keep a full history, so nothing is silently changed.

Can I use it in Swahili?

Yes. The whole app works in English and Swahili, and voice entry understands both, including common Sheng number words.

How do I pay?

Inside the app, with M-Pesa. Choose how many shops and whether to pay daily, weekly or monthly, tap Pay, and enter your M-Pesa PIN when the prompt appears on your phone. Paying early adds the time on top, so you never lose days.

I have more than one shop.

An owner can have several businesses in one account and compare them side by side. Each extra shop costs KSh 29 a day, KSh 179 a week or KSh 699 a month on top of the first.
